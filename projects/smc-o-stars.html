MPhys Research Project

O-Type Stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud

Building and analysing a catalogue of approximately 450 massive stars using Python, uncertainty modelling, and stellar evolutionary models.

Python Pandas NumPy Monte Carlo Statistical Modelling

Overview

This project formed my final-year MPhys research project at the University of Sheffield. I constructed and analysed a catalogue of approximately 450 O-type stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud by combining multiple astronomical datasets.

The project required cleaning and cross-matching heterogeneous data, deriving stellar parameters, propagating measurement uncertainties, comparing different evolutionary models, and analysing the resulting stellar population.

Project at a glance

Dataset: ~450 O-type stars

Primary language: Python

Methods: Monte Carlo simulation, statistical modelling, catalogue cross-matching

Output: Cleaned catalogue, derived stellar parameters, population analysis

The Problem

Massive O-type stars strongly influence the evolution of their host galaxies, but deriving a consistent picture of their properties requires combining information from several different observational catalogues.

The project therefore focused on creating a reliable and internally consistent stellar dataset before using it to investigate the luminosity, mass, age, and spatial distributions of the population.

Analysis Pipeline

01

Data Integration

Cross-matched stellar catalogues and combined spectral classifications, coordinates, and optical photometry.

02

Validation

Identified duplicate and inconsistent entries and applied consistent rules across the full catalogue.

03

Parameter Derivation

Applied luminosity-class-dependent calibrations to estimate effective temperatures, bolometric corrections, and stellar luminosities.

04

Uncertainty Modelling

Propagated photometric, distance, and calibration uncertainties using Monte Carlo simulations.

05

Model Comparison

Compared the derived stellar parameters with multiple evolutionary model grids to estimate masses and ages.

06

Population Analysis

Investigated the HR diagram, mass function, age distribution, and spatial structure of the final sample.
